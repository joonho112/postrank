Posterior Means and Intervals per Entity and Criterion

postrank_posterior_means.Rd

postrank_posterior_means() computes an entity-by-criterion matrix of marginal posterior means, and optionally attaches central equal-tail posterior intervals at level. Intervals summarize marginal target uncertainty one entity and criterion at a time, not simultaneous coverage or intervals for grade numbers.

Usage 

postrank_posterior_means(x, level = NULL)

Arguments

x

A postrank_draws object, a postrank_coefficients object, or a postrank_probabilities() object that stores summaries. Bare postrank_pairwise objects do not determine marginal means and are rejected.

level

NULL (the default, returning means only) or a central interval level in (0, 1). For a postrank_probabilities() input with stored intervals, a non-NULL level must equal x$summaries$level.

Value

An n x K numeric matrix with dimnames entity and criterion. When level is non-NULL, attributes lower, upper, level and interval_source ("draws_equal_tail", "exact_finite_law", "gaussian_analytic", "mixture_analytic" or "supplied_marginal_summaries") are attached.

Details

Calculation method by input type:

  • IID or MCMC draws: equal-weight column means and stats::quantile(..., type = 7);

  • exact finite posterior: probability-weighted means and generalized inverse quantile Q(p)=inf⁡{x:F(x)≥p}Q(p) = \inf\{x : F(x) \ge p\};

  • normal posterior coefficients: stored means and stats::qnorm();

  • shared-state mixture coefficients: stored means and bracketed roots of the marginal mixture CDF verified within probability tolerance 1e-10 (stopping with posterior_quantile_failure if unrepresentable);

  • precomputed probabilities: stored x$summaries without recalculation.

Because means and quantiles are computed separately, a posterior mean can lie outside an equal-tail interval under a skewed distribution.

See also

postrank_report_card(), postrank_composite()
