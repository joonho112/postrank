One or Two Criteria for Six Fictional Sites

pr_tutorial.Rd

pr_tutorial is a synthetic normal-posterior dataset for six fictional sites (A through F) on one criterion (score_A) or two criteria (score_A and score_B). All means and standard deviations are specified directly rather than fitted to real data, providing the introductory examples used in vignette("a1-getting-started", package = "postrank").

Format

A named list with four elements:

one

A postrank_coefficients object with one criterion (score_A) for sites A through F.

two

A postrank_coefficients object for the same six sites on score_A and score_B, keeping the exact same marginal distribution for score_A as one.

units

Data frame of site IDs and display labels in input order.

law

List of the specified means, covariance matrices, conditioning note, priority direction, units and construction metadata.

Source

Synthetic normal distribution specified in data-raw/pr-tutorial-law.json; no external data.

Details

Each site follows an independent bivariate normal distribution with marginal standard deviations 0.2 and zero within-site correlation between score_A and score_B. Larger values mean higher priority on both criteria, and distinct sites have zero probability of exact equality.

Pairwise comparison probabilities and marginal summaries come analytically from the normal distribution, so pr_tutorial contains no posterior draws or Monte Carlo error. Stored grading results for pr_tutorial$one and pr_tutorial$two are available through postrank_example("tutorial_one") and postrank_example("tutorial_two").

See also

postrank_example(), postrank_gaussian_coefficients(), postrank(), vignette("a1-getting-started", package = "postrank")

Examples

names(pr_tutorial)
#> [1] "one"   "two"   "units" "law"  
pr_tutorial$one
#> <postrank_coefficients>  6 entities under a declared conditional law
#>   One criterion: score_A.
#>   Gaussian comparison probabilities and marginal summaries are available.
#>   Supported decisions use weighted loss and an upper limit on grades. Target
#>     draws, q constraints, exact grade counts and minimax decisions are
#>     unsupported here.
#>   i summary(x, details = TRUE) retains the conditional-law declarations and
#>     source records.
postrank_posterior_means(pr_tutorial$one)
#>       criterion
#> entity score_A
#>      A    1.20
#>      B    0.95
#>      C    0.25
#>      D    0.15
#>      E   -0.50
#>      F   -0.80
postrank_posterior_means(pr_tutorial$two)
#>       criterion
#> entity score_A score_B
#>      A    1.20     0.7
#>      B    0.95     0.4
#>      C    0.25     0.9
#>      D    0.15     0.6
#>      E   -0.50    -0.5
#>      F   -0.80    -0.7
