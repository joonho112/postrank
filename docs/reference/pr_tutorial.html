One or Two Criteria for Six Fictional Sites

pr_tutorial.Rd

This Gaussian example illustrates the disclosure and withholding of pairwise comparisons through integer grades. All means and variances are specified directly, so the posterior laws are illustrative rather than fitted to observations.

Format

A named list with four elements:

one

A postrank_coefficients input with one criterion, score_A, for sites A through F.

two

A postrank_coefficients input for the same six sites with score_A and score_B. The law of score_A is identical to that in one.

units

Site IDs and display labels in input order.

law

The fixed means, covariance matrices, conditioning statement, direction, units and maintainer source information.

Source

Maintainer-created synthetic law in data-raw/pr-tutorial-law.json; no external observations.

Details

Each site has a bivariate normal law. Both marginal standard deviations are 0.2. The two criteria are independent within each site, and sites are independent. Higher values have higher priority on both criteria. Scores are in illustrative standardized units with no empirical calibration. Continuous normal laws have zero equality probability.

Analytic comparison probabilities and marginal summaries are calculated from the same specified law. The example contains no draws, fitted-model uncertainty or Monte Carlo diagnostics. Addition of the second criterion changes the decision problem while retaining the first criterion's marginal law.

Examples

names(pr_tutorial)
#> [1] "one"   "two"   "units" "law"  
pr_tutorial$one
#> <postrank_coefficients>  6 entities under a declared conditional law
#>   One criterion: score_A.
#>   Gaussian comparison probabilities and marginal summaries are available.
#>   Supported decisions use weighted loss and an upper limit on grades. Target
#>     draws, q constraints, exact grade counts and minimax decisions are
#>     unsupported here.
#>   i summary(x, details = TRUE) retains the conditional-law declarations and
#>     source records.
pr_tutorial$two
#> <postrank_coefficients>  6 entities under a declared conditional law
#>   2 criteria: score_A, score_B.
#>   Gaussian comparison probabilities and marginal summaries are available.
#>   Supported decisions use weighted loss and an upper limit on grades. Target
#>     draws, q constraints, exact grade counts and minimax decisions are
#>     unsupported here.
#>   i summary(x, details = TRUE) retains the conditional-law declarations and
#>     source records.
postrank_posterior_means(pr_tutorial$one)
#>       criterion
#> entity score_A
#>      A    1.20
#>      B    0.95
#>      C    0.25
#>      D    0.15
#>      E   -0.50
#>      F   -0.80
postrank_posterior_means(pr_tutorial$two)
#>       criterion
#> entity score_A score_B
#>      A    1.20     0.7
#>      B    0.95     0.4
#>      C    0.25     0.9
#>      D    0.15     0.6
#>      E   -0.50    -0.5
#>      F   -0.80    -0.7
