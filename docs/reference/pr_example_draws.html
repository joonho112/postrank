An Additional Three-Criterion Example with 4000 IID Draws

pr_example_draws.Rd

A validated joint postrank_draws object with all eight scientific declarations. A synthetic dataset was generated from a Gaussian model (K = 3, latent covariance .4 I + .6 s s' with s = (1, 1, -1), cyclic precision groups of ten sites) and passed through the known-Gaussian posterior; the resulting law (30 x 3 means, 3 x 3 x 30 covariances) was frozen and 4000 IID draws were generated from it once (seed 20261024). The criteria are named poverty, frl and snap for illustration only; the values are synthetic latent needs, not real measures. The same law is shipped as pr_example_gaussian.

Usage

pr_example_draws

Format

A postrank_draws object whose joint_draws$tensor$values is a 4000 x 30 x 3 array (draw, entity site001-site030, criterion poverty, frl, snap); the remaining fields follow the legacy schema.

Source

data-raw/make-pr-example-law.R (law stage, seed 20261023) and data-raw/make-pr-example-draws.R (draw stage, seed 20261024); the frozen law and configuration are data-raw/pr-example-law.json and data-raw/pr-example-config.json.

Details

The snap criterion is negatively related to the other two criteria, and the posteriors are imprecise. Under the illustrative policy weights = c(poverty = .5, frl = .25, snap = .25), G_max = 4, the validated incumbent at tau = .25 is the all-withheld grading (every comparison withheld, risk tau), and at tau = .35 it discloses about 63% of the pairs in four grades. Both results are provided as cached results for the vignettes.

dim(pr_example_draws$joint_draws$tensor$values)
#> [1] 4000   30    3
