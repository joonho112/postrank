Extract Posterior Loss with Its Solver Bound and Status

get_risk.Rd

get_risk() returns the posterior expected loss (risk) of the checked grading in x together with the solver's lower bound across all allowed gradings, optimization gap and normalized termination status.

Usage

get_risk(x, ...)

Arguments

x

A postrank_fit or postrank_analysis object.

...

Unused arguments for generic compatibility.

Value

A named list with four elements:

  • risk: posterior expected loss of the checked grading (weighted sum of criterion losses when objective == "weighted", or largest selected criterion loss when objective == "minimax"), or NA_real_ when no checked grading is available;

  • lower_bound: a loss that no allowed grading for x's problem can beat (up to solver and floating-point tolerances), or NA_real_ when no usable lower bound is available;

  • gap: risk - lower_bound, or NA_real_ when either is NA;

  • status: normalized solver status string ("optimal", "feasible_not_certified", "time_limit", "infeasible", "unbounded", "numerical_error" or "unknown").

Details

lower_bound, gap and status describe optimization over the supplied pairwise probabilities only; they do not measure posterior uncertainty, Monte Carlo error, or excess loss ("regret") under the true posterior when probabilities are estimated from finite draws.

When a solver stops at its time limit without a checked grading, risk and gap are NA_real_ while status and any usable lower_bound are kept (distinct from a valid one-grade report leaving all pairs unordered, which has risk = tau). Even with a checked grading, lower_bound and gap are NA_real_ if no bound was computed or if the reported bound was excluded on numerical checks; inspect objective$bound_source (on a postrank_fit, or x$fit$objective$bound_source on a postrank_analysis) and x$warnings for details.

See also

get_profile(), get_grades(), postrank_solve()

Examples

one <- postrank_example("tutorial_one")
get_risk(one)
#> $risk
#> [1] 0.04165164
#> 
#> $lower_bound
#> [1] 0.04165164
#> 
#> $gap
#> [1] 0
#> 
#> $status
#> [1] "optimal"
#>
