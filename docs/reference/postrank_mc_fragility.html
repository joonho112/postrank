Find Pairwise Decisions Near the Monte Carlo Noise Scale

postrank_mc_fragility.Rd

postrank_mc_fragility() identifies unordered entity pairs whose estimated wrong-direction error in the better ordering direction lies close to the withholding cost tau relative to its Monte Carlo standard error (MCSE), without running a solver. Because grades are chosen jointly across all pairs, a flagged pair need not change the selected grading.

Usage 

postrank_mc_fragility(
  draws,
  fit = NULL,
  settings = NULL,
  z = 2,
  screen_margin = NULL
)

Arguments

draws

Joint equal-weight posterior draws from postrank_draws() or as_postrank_draws() (dependence_mode = "joint_model"). Criterion-wise blocks, exact finite laws, Gaussian or mixture coefficients, pre-computed probabilities, and tie-mode inputs are not accepted.

fit, settings

Supply fit (postrank_fit or postrank_analysis on draws) or settings (postrank_settings() or named list with weights and tau), not both. When fit has a checked grading, each traced pair is also labeled by its relation in fit$grade.

z

Positive numeric MCSE multiplier (2 by default) setting the descriptive flagging threshold |d - tau| < z * mcse.

screen_margin

Positive numeric pre-screen threshold: only pairs with |d - tau| < screen_margin have per-draw indicator traces built. When NULL (the default), IID inputs with S > 1 use z * 0.5 / sqrt(S - 1) (the maximum sample SD of a [0, 1] indicator, so no pair that could be flagged is skipped), and MCMC inputs use 0.1 (a working screen accompanied by a warning, not an upper bound).

Value

A postrank_mc_fragility list with pairs (data frame of traced pairs containing entity_i, entity_j, better_direction, weighted_error, margin, disclose_preferred, mcse, ratio, and fragile, plus relation_in_grade when fit has grades), summary (pairs, traced, fragile, fragile_share, mcse_unavailable, plus fragile_disclosed and fragile_tied when fit has grades), regime, draws, z, screen_margin, mcse_method, and warnings.

Details

For each unordered pair (i, j), let e‾ij=∑kwkeij,k\bar e_{ij} = \sum_k w_k e_{ij,k} be the criterion-weighted directional-error probability and d=min⁡(e‾ij,e‾ji)d = \min(\bar e_{ij}, \bar e_{ji}). Taken in isolation, ordering the pair in its better direction beats withholding when d<τd < \tau. For pairs passing screen_margin, postrank_mc_fragility() builds the per-draw weighted indicator trace for that empirically better direction and flags |d - tau| < z * mcse.

IID MCSE is sd(trace) / sqrt(S). MCMC MCSE uses posterior::mcse_mean() on equal-length chains; unequal chain lengths or an uninstalled posterior package leave mcse and fragile as NA with a warning rather than truncating chains or substituting zero. Pairwise fragility flags are descriptive summaries and do not assess global grade stability, account for G_max or error budgets q, or provide simultaneous confidence levels or a finite-sample guarantee.

  postrank_toy,
  settings = list(weights = c(need_A = 0.6, need_B = 0.4), tau = 0.25)
