Historical Economic Opportunity in 67 Alabama Counties

pr_case_alabama.Rd

pr_case_alabama is a two-criterion case-study dataset for all 67 Alabama counties adapted from the Opportunity Atlas replication data, combining adult household income rank (measured in 2014–2015) and incarceration rate (measured on April 1, 2010) for children born in 1978–1983 to parents at the 25th national income percentile (race and gender pooled). Larger target values mean higher adult income and lower incarceration. These values describe historical cohort outcomes, not current county conditions or causal policy effects.

Usage

pr_case_alabama

Format

A named list with five elements:

input

A postrank_probabilities object for the 67 counties on income and incarceration from MCMC pool A, with marginal posterior means and 95% equal-tail intervals.

units

Data frame of 67 rows in input order: county IDs and names, published Opportunity Atlas point estimates and standard errors, and oriented point-scale columns. Published cohort counts are descriptive, not binomial denominators in the model.

alternatives

Alternative postrank_probabilities inputs: pool_B (from a second MCMC run on the same data and model) and single-criterion income and incarceration from pool A, each with matching 95% marginal summaries.

reference

Archived settings, seven reference gradings, pool A and B loss evaluations, single-criterion and commonality summaries, and chain-based precision records (originally computed with postrank 0.9.0 and HiGHS 1.12.0.3).

provenance

Source filenames and SHA-256 hashes, cohort and outcome dates, target orientations, model conditioning, attribution and the CC BY 4.0 license notice.

Source

Chetty, Raj, John Friedman, Nathaniel Hendren, Maggie R. Jones and Sonya R. Porter (2022), Replication Data for: The Opportunity Atlas: Mapping the Childhood Roots of Social Mobility, Harvard Dataverse V1.0, doi:10.7910/DVN/NKCQM1 ; Opportunity Insights and U.S. Census Bureau (county_outcomes_simple.csv, licensed under CC BY 4.0). Alabama filtering, county labels, incarceration sign orientation, separate hierarchical fits and compact probability extraction by the package author; no endorsement is implied.

Details

Model and numerical pools. Each criterion was fitted separately to the published county point estimates and standard errors using a Gaussian observation model with logistic-normal partial pooling. The resulting pairwise probabilities support weighted additive expected loss without specifying a cross-criterion joint posterior, and observed Monte Carlo 0 or 1 frequencies are finite-sample estimates rather than exact posterior certainties.

Pool A (16,000 retained MCMC draws per criterion in the source study) and pool B (8,000 draws) are two MCMC runs on the exact same observations and model, not independent data replications. Original MCMC draws are not bundled, so archived chain-based MCSEs cannot be recomputed from input (and MCSE on constant zero/one pair traces is unavailable, not zero). In pool B, 486 directional criterion entries exceeded the source study's 0.005 pair-MCSE screen (maximum 0.00586708).

Intervals and archived settings. Pass level = 0.95 when requesting a report card in postrank() or postrank_report_card() (other levels cannot be computed from stored summaries). Incarceration is negated so lower incarceration means higher priority toward grade 1. At weights = c(0.5, 0.5), tau = 0.20 and G_max = 5 (see postrank_example("case_alabama67")), the common grading matches the expected-rank cut baseline B3, whose fixed-order optimality has a narrower scope than the unrestricted solver lower bound.

Source license. Source observations and documented derivatives (including dependent stored results) are distributed under CC BY 4.0, with attribution and modification notices retained in provenance$rights (alongside historical preparation records) and the installed DATA-NOTICE.md.
