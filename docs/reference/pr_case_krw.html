Race and Gender Contact Gaps at 97 Employers

pr_case_krw.Rd

pr_case_krw is a two-criterion case-study dataset for 97 U.S. employers adapted from the replication archive of Kline, Rose and Walters (2024), A Discrimination Report Card. Its primary input compares all 97 firms on signed race and gender callback-gap targets: larger race_signed means a larger white-name callback advantage (log(p_white) - log(p_black)), and larger gender_signed means a larger male-name callback advantage (log(p_male) - log(p_female), signed rather than folded in magnitude).

Usage

pr_case_krw

Format

A named list with five elements:

input

A postrank_probabilities object with a 97 x 97 x 2 directional-error array and matching 97 x 2 posterior means and 90% equal-tail intervals.

units

Data frame of 97 rows in input order: entity_id, firm_id, firm_name, industry, race_estimate, race_se, gender_estimate and gender_se (direct log-contact-gap estimates and standard errors, not posterior means or standard deviations).

alternatives

Named list of postrank_probabilities inputs: single-criterion race and gender, and two-criterion absolute_gender (replacing gender_signed with |gender_signed| folded across zero).

reference

KRW single-criterion report cards (single_criterion), companion two-criterion common-grading references (signed_core and absolute_sensitivity), cell-law and archive-matrix loss summaries, raw and plotting Pi arrays, and summary counts of solved specification-grid cells.

provenance

Source filenames and SHA-256 hashes, posterior cell-law construction, attribution and the source CC-BY-NC rights notice.

Source

Kline, Patrick, Evan K. Rose and Christopher R. Walters (2024), Replication Data for: A Discrimination Report Card (titled Data and Code for: A Discrimination Report Card on openICPSR), American Economic Association / ICPSR deposit E198284V1, doi:10.3886/E198284V1 . Midpoint cell-law probabilities and compact extraction by data-raw/make-pr-case-krw.R (no endorsement by the original authors is implied).

Details

Posterior cell law. Pairwise probabilities and marginal summaries treat each firm's posterior mass on the replication grid as uniform over continuous cells between adjacent grid midpoints (with nonnegative support on the lowest race cell), holding the archive's empirical-Bayes prior fixed without refitting or normal approximation. Pass level = 0.90 when requesting a report card in postrank() or postrank_report_card(). No posterior draws or cross-criterion joint distribution are stored.

Reference settings. Kline, Rose and Walters (2024) report single-criterion report cards (single_criterion) at benchmark preference parameter lambda = 0.25 (tau = 0.20) allowing up to 97 grades (3 occupied grades on race and 4 on gender without industry controls). The two-criterion common gradings (signed_core and postrank_example("case_krw_signed97")) are postrank companion analyses using tau = 0.25, equal weights (0.5, 0.5) and G_max = 4. Stored reference actions are evaluated under the midpoint-cell law (not a new solve or exact optimality claim over alternative laws) and describe the historical correspondence experiment rather than current firm practices.

Source license. The replication archive README states: "The data are licensed under a Creative Commons/CC-BY-NC license." without naming a license version. pr_case_krw and dependent stored results retain that noncommercial CC-BY-NC condition separately from the package's MIT code license (provenance$rights retains historical preparation records, while the installed DATA-NOTICE.md states the current public distribution scope).
