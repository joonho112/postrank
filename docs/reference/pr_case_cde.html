Chronic Absence in a Small California School Example

pr_case_cde.Rd

pr_case_cde is a one-criterion case-study dataset for 12 California elementary schools adapted from 2026 California School Dashboard chronic-absenteeism counts (academic year 2025–26) and an empirical-Bayes model. Larger target values mean lower chronic-absence probability: θ=−p\theta = -p, measured in proportion units. Source attribution and documented adaptations are recorded in provenance and the installed DATA-NOTICE.md file.

Usage 

pr_case_cde

Format

A named list with five elements:

input

A postrank_probabilities object for the 12 schools on one criterion, with marginal posterior means and 90% equal-tail intervals for θ=−p\theta = -p.

units

Data frame of 12 rows in input order with columns entity_id (14-digit County-District-School CDS codes stored as character strings to preserve leading zeros), chronic_absent_count, eligible_count, observed_rate, observed_rate_group and denominator_group. Counts are observed school data, not posterior estimates.

alternatives

Alternative postrank_probabilities inputs order_32 and order_64, computed with 32 and 64 Gauss-Legendre nodes per prior bin under the same posterior and marginal summaries.

reference

Settings, three fixed comparison gradings (all-unordered, observed-rate tertiles and posterior-mean tertiles) with their losses, piecewise-Beta posterior parameters, quadrature diagnostics, and archived statewide baseline summaries.

provenance

Source filenames and SHA-256 hashes, dates, school selection rule, model conditioning, target orientation, attribution and historical preparation-stage review records. Raw CDE download files, logos, full source tables and statewide pairwise matrices are not bundled.

Source

California Department of Education, 2026 California School Dashboard chronic absenteeism data, academic year 2025–26, https://www.cde.ca.gov/ta/ac/cm/chronicdatafiles.asp; source conditions at https://www.cde.ca.gov/re/di/cr/index.asp (accessed 2026-10-30). Empirical-Bayes model, numerical integration and 12-school selection by the package author; no CDE endorsement is implied.

Details

School selection. The statewide frame comprises 5,834 elementary-type schools outside Dashboard Alternative School Status (non-DASS, including eligible charter schools) with usable ALL-student chronic-absence counts and denominators of at least 30. One school was chosen from each cell of 4 observed-rate rank groups crossed with 3 denominator rank groups (the lower-median row ceiling(m / 2) within each cell when ordered by observed rate, denominator and CDS code). This 12-school subset spans rate and size strata for teaching and is not a representative sample of California schools.

Posterior and numerical integration. A piecewise-uniform empirical-Bayes prior G over 161 continuous bins was estimated on the 5,834-school frame and held fixed; uncertainty in estimating G is not propagated. Within each bin, a school's posterior is a truncated Beta(y + 1, n - y + 1) density weighted by its posterior bin probability, so independent school posteriors given G have zero probability of exact equality. Primary pairwise probabilities use 16 Gauss-Legendre nodes per bin, clipping raw values that overshot [0, 1] by at most 64 machine epsilons back into [0, 1] before applying symmetric complement correction with tolerance = 1e-6 in postrank_probabilities(). Small corrections and agreement across 16, 32 and 64 nodes are diagnostic checks, not certified bounds on integration error, and no posterior draws are stored.

Intervals and scope. Each school's mean is −E[p]-E[p] and its 90% marginal (not simultaneous) interval is [−q0.95(p),−q0.05(p)][-q_{0.95}(p), -q_{0.05}(p)]; pass level = 0.90 in postrank() or postrank_report_card() (other levels cannot be computed from stored summaries). Archived statewide rows in reference$statewide$metrics summarize fixed-order contiguous cuts, an all-unordered row and matched grade-size baselines rather than an unrestricted 5,834-school optimization, and their independent-pair relaxation lower bound has a separate scope. Posterior-conditioned losses do not provide external validation, and the results do not reproduce California Dashboard Status-plus-Change colors or estimate causal effects.

Source conditions. Only the 12 selected school IDs/counts and author-derived summaries are included. provenance$rights retains historical preparation-stage records, while the installed DATA-NOTICE.md describes the current public distribution scope. CDE source conditions apply to pr_case_cde and dependent stored results (such as postrank_example("case_cde_12")) rather than a Creative Commons or MIT code license.
